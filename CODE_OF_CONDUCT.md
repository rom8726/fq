# Code of Conduct

fq should be a practical, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before escalating disagreement.
- Keep discussions focused on the work and its impact.
- Respect maintainers' time by providing reproducible details and concise context.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of issues, pull requests, or maintainer decisions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or delete comments; close issues or pull requests;
or block participants whose behavior harms the project.

If you experience or notice unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately
through the repository owner profile. Reports will be handled with care and kept as
confidential as practical.
